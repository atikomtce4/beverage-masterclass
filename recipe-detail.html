Roselle Juice

These easy recipes juice with easy drink it very fresh and healthy

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 15 mins
Servings 10 Liters

Ingredients

  • Water 10 liters
  • Dried Roselle flowers 500g
  • Brown sugar 800g (or Stevia 50g)
  • Salt 1 tsp

Instructions

1
Fill water 10 liters into the pot and heat to 95°C
2
Add dried roselle flowers and stir gently
3
Maintain temperature at 90-95°C for 15 minutes
4
Strain, add sugar and salt, stir until dissolved
